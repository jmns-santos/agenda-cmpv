Help

Google

Tap Subscribe: Google Calendar opens in the Google account you are signed in to. Confirm to add it. The calendar shows on every device linked to that account.

Outlook

With a Microsoft 365 account (work or school), Subscribe opens Outlook on the web and asks you to confirm.

With a personal account (Outlook.com, Hotmail), choose “Other”, copy the link and, on outlook.com, add a calendar from the web.

iPhone, iPad or Mac

On the iPhone, iPad or Mac itself, tap Subscribe and confirm in Calendar. On a Windows computer this button does not work: choose Google or Outlook.

If nothing happens (for example, when the page is open inside another app), open it in Safari, or choose “Other”, copy the link and add it in Calendar settings as a subscribed calendar.

Android

Tap Subscribe: the calendar is added to your Google account and shows in Google Calendar and Samsung Calendar. If it does not show, open Google Calendar settings, tap the calendar name and turn on sync.

Other calendar

Tap “Copy link” and paste the link into your app, in the option to subscribe to or add a calendar from URL (Thunderbird: new calendar on the network; Proton, Yahoo, Nextcloud and others: calendar from URL).

How do I get a reminder before an event?

iPhone, iPad or Mac: before subscribing, choose the reminder (1 day or 1 week before each event). Calendar usually removes reminders when subscribing: turn off “Remove Alerts” in that calendar's settings.

Google and Android: after subscribing, open Google Calendar settings, choose the calendar and, under “Event notifications”, add the reminder (for example, 1 week). It applies to every event in that calendar.

Outlook and other apps: subscribed calendars do not allow reminders. In “Upcoming events”, tap “Add to my calendar”: the event becomes yours and you can choose the reminder.

Where can I buy tickets or register?

When the event page shows prices, free entry, tickets or registration, that information appears in the event notes, with the ticket link (for example, BOL) or registration link. Always check the event page.

How are the theme and type chosen?

Automatically, from the title and description of each event. Each event has a single theme and a single type and is always in the full calendar. In some cases the classification may not be the best fit.

Which language are the events in?

This page and the names of calendars and categories are in the language you choose. The title and description of each event stay in Portuguese, as on the municipality's website. Dates and times follow your phone's format.

A calendar you already subscribed to does not change language: to change it, delete it and subscribe again on this page, in the language you want.

How often is it updated?

Events are read from the municipality's website every 4 hours. Each app decides when to check the calendar again: it may take a few hours and, in Google Calendar, up to a day.

How to unsubscribe

Delete the subscribed calendar in your app's settings. The events disappear immediately.

Events come from the events page at cm-pvarzim.pt. Check dates, times and access conditions on each event page (the link is in the event notes).
